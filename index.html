About Me

Hey, my name is James Lumber. I am a first year student at Queen's University, Belfast. I am currently studying for a Masters in Software Engineering. This website is a showcase of extra-curricular projects that I have created, to improve my skills in various areas of computer science, alongside my studies.

So about me, for my hobbies and interests, I enjoy playing football, basketball, and computer games in my free time. The latter is what really lit the spark for my great interest in the field of Computer Science as a whole. Within this field, I am interested in a wide variety of computer related topics. However, the main three topics that pique my interest are Cryptography, Financial Technology, and Artificial Intelligence.

In terms of my prior education, I undertook my A-Levels and GCSEs at St Mary's Grammar School Magherafelt. I achieved 7A*Business Studies, Digital Technology (Programming), Double Award Science, English Language, Further Mathematics, and Geography and 3A'sEnglish Literature, Irish Language, and Mathematics in my GCSEs, and 1A*Mathematics and 3A'sEconomics, Physics, and Software Systems Development in my A-Levels. Additionally, I was top in my school for Further Mathematics at GCSE, and Mathematics at AS Level.

Concerning experience, in the working world, I have 3 years of retail experience. More importantly than that though, I had a 3-month internship in the IT department of one of Northern Ireland's largest construction companies, Henry Brothers LTD. During this placement period, I had the privileged opportunity to see behind the scenes of the day to day running of the business' IT department, as well as aid them in various tasks to get hands-on experience.
